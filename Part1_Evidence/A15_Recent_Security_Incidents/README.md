# A15 — Recent Security Incidents

# Objective
The purpose of this activity is to investigate recent cybersecurity incidents across multiple years and industries to understand their causes, impacts, and lessons learned.

# Incident 1: SolarWinds Supply Chain Attack
**Year:** 2020  
**Description:** Hackers compromised SolarWinds software updates, allowing malicious code to infiltrate thousands of organizations, including government agencies.  
**Impact:** Extensive global exposure of sensitive data.  
**Lesson Learned:** Supply chain attacks are extremely dangerous; software trust validation is critical.

# Incident 2: Colonial Pipeline Ransomware Attack
**Year:** 2021  
**Description:** The Colonial Pipeline network was shut down by ransomware, disrupting fuel supply along the US East Coast.  
**Impact:** Fuel shortages and panic buying across multiple states.  
**Lesson Learned:** Critical infrastructure must maintain strong backup strategies and robust incident response plans.

# Incident 3: Asahi Data Breach
**Year:** 2022  
**Description:** Personal data of over 2 million users was exposed due to a breach of Asahi’s corporate systems.  
**Impact:** Large-scale compromise of sensitive user data.  
**Lesson Learned:** Corporations must implement strong cybersecurity policies and monitor for insider threats and external attacks.

# Incident 4: Bybit Hack (Largest Crypto Heist)
**Year:** 2023  
**Description:** Hackers exploited platform vulnerabilities to steal a record amount of cryptocurrency from Bybit users.  
**Impact:** Millions of dollars of crypto assets were stolen.  
**Lesson Learned:** Even highly technical platforms are vulnerable; continuous auditing and threat detection are critical.

# Incident 5: MOVEit Transfer Vulnerability
**Year:** 2023
**Description:** A zero-day vulnerability in MOVEit Transfer software was exploited to steal sensitive files from multiple organizations.  
**Impact:** Sensitive corporate data exposed, highlighting risks of unpatched systems.  
**Lesson Learned:** Timely patching and monitoring for zero-day exploits are crucial for secure operations.

# Overall Reflection
Through this activity, I learned that cybersecurity incidents span industries and years, highlighting the importance of proactive security, patching, monitoring, and user awareness. Each incident provided insight into how vulnerabilities can be exploited and emphasized that both technical measures and organizational policies are essential for robust cybersecurity.
