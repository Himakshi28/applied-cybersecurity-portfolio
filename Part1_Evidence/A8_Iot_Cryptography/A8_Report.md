Activity A8 – Cryptography in Internet of Things Devices

I investigated how cryptography is used in Internet of Things (IoT)
devices to protect user data and communications.

Examples analysed included Apple Watch, Google Nest, and Ring Camera.
Official documentation showed the use of encryption, secure authentication,
and protected communication channels.

This activity demonstrated how cryptographic techniques are embedded
in everyday connected devices to ensure confidentiality, integrity,
and secure access.
