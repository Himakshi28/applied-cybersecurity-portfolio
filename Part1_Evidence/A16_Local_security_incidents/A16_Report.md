# Activity A16 – Discover 3 Local Security Incidents

# Overview
This activity investigates real cybersecurity incidents occurring in Australia to understand how security failures impact organisations and individuals.

# Incident 1: IVF Clinic Data Leak
Sensitive patient data from a major IVF clinic was reportedly leaked on the dark web. The breach exposed confidential medical information, highlighting risks associated with healthcare data storage and access control failures.

**Security concepts observed:**
- Data confidentiality failure
- Unauthorized access
- Dark web data exposure

# Incident 2: Harry Perkins Institute Cybersecurity Breach
The Harry Perkins Institute of Medical Research investigated a significant cybersecurity incident affecting research systems. This demonstrates risks faced by research organisations handling sensitive intellectual property and health data.

**Security concepts observed:**
- Network intrusion
- Incident response investigation
- Research data protection challenges

# Incident 3: Commonwealth Bank Mortgage Fraud Investigation
Commonwealth Bank reported a potential $1 billion mortgage fraud scheme to authorities. This incident highlights financial system security risks and the importance of fraud detection mechanisms.

**Security concepts observed:**
- Financial fraud detection
- Internal monitoring controls
- Regulatory reporting obligations

# Reflection
These incidents demonstrate that cybersecurity threats affect multiple sectors including healthcare, research, and finance. Effective monitoring, encryption, and access control mechanisms are essential to mitigate such risks.

Screenshots of verified news sources are included as supporting evidence.
