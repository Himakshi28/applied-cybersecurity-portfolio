# A1 – Physical Security Controls Observation

# Objective
The purpose of this activity is to identify and document physical security controls used in a public environment to protect people, assets, and information.

# Location
University campus / public study area.

# Observed Security Controls

# 1. CCTV Camera
CCTV cameras are installed to monitor activities and deter unauthorized or suspicious behaviour. They help in incident investigation and improve overall safety.

# 2. Security Camera
Security cameras provide continuous surveillance of the area and help monitor entrances and public spaces.

# 3. Fire Extinguisher
Fire extinguishers are placed in accessible locations to respond quickly to fire emergencies and reduce damage or risk to individuals.

# 4. Card Tap Entry Lock
Card-based access control systems restrict entry to authorized individuals only, preventing unauthorized access to secured areas.

# Evidence Collected
Screenshots/photos were taken of:
- CCTV camera
- Security camera
- Fire extinguisher
- Card tap entry lock

# Reflection
This activity helped me understand how physical security controls work together to protect facilities and individuals. Multiple layers of protection improve safety and reduce risks.

# Conclusion
Physical security measures are an important part of cybersecurity because protecting physical access also protects digital systems and sensitive information.
