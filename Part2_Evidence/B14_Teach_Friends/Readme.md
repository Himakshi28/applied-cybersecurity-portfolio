# B14 — Teach Your Friends About Cybersecurity

## Objective
The purpose of this activity is to educate friends about important cybersecurity practices and increase awareness of online safety.

---

## Description
Cybersecurity advice was shared with friends through online messaging platforms. The discussion focused on common cybersecurity risks and safe online practices.

Topics discussed included:
- strong passwords,
- multi-factor authentication (MFA),
- phishing awareness,
- suspicious links,
- password reuse,
- and public Wi-Fi risks.

## Outcome
This activity demonstrated the importance of spreading cybersecurity awareness among peers and encouraging safer online behavior.

---

## Conclusion
Cybersecurity education is important for reducing risks and helping individuals recognize common online threats and scams.
