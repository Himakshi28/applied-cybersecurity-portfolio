# B7 — Participate in a CS/DS/Cybersecurity Club Activity

## Objective
The purpose of this activity is to engage with cybersecurity-related student community activities and improve awareness of cybersecurity collaboration opportunities.

---

## Description
Cybersecurity and computing club activities were reviewed to understand student engagement opportunities, cybersecurity workshops, and community participation.

---

## Topics Observed
- Cybersecurity awareness
- Technical workshops
- Industry networking
- Security competitions
- Student collaboration

---

## Outcome
This activity demonstrated awareness of cybersecurity student communities and opportunities for collaborative cybersecurity learning.

---

## Conclusion
Cybersecurity clubs and student communities help improve technical skills, awareness, collaboration, and industry engagement.
