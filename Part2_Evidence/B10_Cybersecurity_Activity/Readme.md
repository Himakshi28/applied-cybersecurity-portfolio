# B10 — Participate in a Cybersecurity Activity as Part of Volunteering or Community Assistance

## Objective
The purpose of this activity is to participate in a cybersecurity-related awareness and assistance activity.

---

## Description
Cybersecurity awareness assistance was provided to university students and peers through cybersecurity education and awareness activities.

The activity focused on:
- phishing awareness,
- password safety,
- multi-factor authentication (MFA),
- and safe online practices.

---

## Tasks Performed
- Shared cybersecurity awareness advice
- Explained phishing attack risks
- Recommended strong password practices
- Encouraged MFA usage
- Created cybersecurity awareness materials

---

## Outcome
This activity demonstrated participation in practical cybersecurity awareness and educational support activities.

---

## Conclusion
Cybersecurity awareness activities help improve digital safety and encourage safer online behavior within the community.
