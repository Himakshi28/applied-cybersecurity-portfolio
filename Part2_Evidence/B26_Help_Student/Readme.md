# B26 — Help Another Student Learn a Cybersecurity Concept

## Objective
The purpose of this activity is to help another student understand an important cybersecurity concept.

---

## Description
Cybersecurity advice and explanations were shared with another student through online communication.

The topic discussed was phishing attacks, including:
- recognizing phishing emails,
- avoiding suspicious links,
- verifying senders,
- and enabling multi-factor authentication (MFA).

---

---

## Outcome
This activity demonstrated the importance of peer learning and cybersecurity awareness among students.

---

## Conclusion
Helping others understand cybersecurity concepts contributes to safer online behavior and strengthens overall cybersecurity awareness.
