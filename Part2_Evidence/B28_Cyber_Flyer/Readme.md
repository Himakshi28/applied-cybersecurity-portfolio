# B28 — Produce a Cyber Safety Flyer for University Students

## Objective
The purpose of this activity is to design a cyber safety awareness flyer targeted at university students.

---

## Description
A cybersecurity awareness flyer was created to educate university students about common cybersecurity risks and safe online practices.

The flyer included practical security recommendations such as:
- using strong passwords,
- enabling MFA,
- avoiding unsecured Wi-Fi,
- updating devices regularly,
- and identifying phishing attacks.

---

## Design Process
The flyer was designed using Canva and included security-themed visuals and awareness messages.

---


---

## Outcome
This activity demonstrated the importance of cybersecurity awareness and communication skills when educating others about online safety.

---

## Conclusion
Cybersecurity awareness is an essential part of modern digital life. Educational materials such as flyers help promote safer online behavior among university students.
