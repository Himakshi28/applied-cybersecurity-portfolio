# B15 — Teach an Elderly Person About Cybersecurity

## Objective
The purpose of this activity is to educate an elderly person about important cybersecurity and online safety practices.

---

## Description
Cybersecurity awareness advice was shared with an elderly individual through online communication.

Topics discussed included:
- scam calls,
- phishing messages,
- suspicious links,
- OTP/password safety,
- and software updates.

The goal was to improve awareness of common cyber threats that frequently target elderly individuals.

---

---

## Outcome
This activity demonstrated the importance of cybersecurity education and awareness for vulnerable groups such as elderly individuals.

---

## Conclusion
Educating elderly users about cyber threats helps reduce the risk of scams, fraud, and online attacks while promoting safer digital practices.
