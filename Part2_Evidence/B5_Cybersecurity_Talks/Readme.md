# B5 — Attend Cybersecurity Related Talks/Seminars

## Objective
The purpose of this activity is to improve cybersecurity awareness and knowledge by attending and reviewing cybersecurity-related talks, webinars, and seminars.

---

## Description
Cybersecurity-related webinars and online talks were reviewed to gain knowledge about modern cyber threats, cyber resilience, and cybersecurity practices used by organizations and security professionals.

---

## Talk/Seminar 1 — CyberCX Webinar Series

### Topic
Cybersecurity webinars focusing on cyber risk management, resilience, and cybersecurity awareness.

### Key Learning
- Importance of cyber resilience
- Managing cybersecurity risks
- Security awareness in modern organizations
- Understanding evolving cyber threats`

---

## Talk/Seminar 2 — How to Bounce Back from a Cyber Attack

### Topic
Discussion about how organizations respond to and recover from cyber attacks.

### Key Learning
- Incident response strategies
- Cyber attack recovery planning
- Communication during cyber incidents
- Lessons learned from real cyber attack case studies`

---

## Outcome
This activity improved understanding of real-world cybersecurity challenges, cyber resilience, and incident response practices.

---

## Conclusion
Cybersecurity talks and webinars are valuable learning opportunities that improve awareness of cyber threats, organizational security practices, and modern cybersecurity strategies.
